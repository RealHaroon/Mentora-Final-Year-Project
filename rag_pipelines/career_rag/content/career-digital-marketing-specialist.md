career: "Digital Marketing Specialist"

A Digital Marketing Specialist plans and runs campaigns that get a
product or brand in front of the right audience online — across search,
social media, content, and paid advertising — and measures what actually
works rather than relying on guesswork. The role blends creative work
(writing, campaign ideas) with genuinely analytical work (reading
performance data and adjusting strategy based on it).

**Typical required skills:** SEO (search engine optimization — getting
content found organically), content strategy (planning what to publish
and why, not just writing it), Google Analytics or a similar tool for
measuring real campaign performance, social media marketing across the
platforms relevant to a given audience, copywriting, and A/B testing —
running controlled experiments to find out which version of a message or
page actually performs better rather than assuming.

**Typical entry path:** a business, marketing, or communications
background is common but not required — many digital marketers are
self-taught through free certifications (Google Analytics, Google Ads,
HubSpot) combined with real hands-on practice managing an actual
campaign, even a small personal or freelance one. Being able to show real
before/after metrics from a campaign you ran is more convincing to
employers than a credential alone.

**A realistic first 6 months on the job** involves much more routine
reporting and iteration on existing campaigns than the creative
"big idea" work most newcomers expect — a large share of the actual work
is reading last week's numbers and making small, data-driven adjustments.
