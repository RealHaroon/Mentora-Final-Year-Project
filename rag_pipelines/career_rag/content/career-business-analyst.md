career: "Business Analyst"

A Business Analyst bridges business needs and data — gathering
requirements from stakeholders, analyzing existing processes or data, and
recommending or supporting decisions, usually without needing to write
production code or build models from scratch. The role is more about
asking the right questions and presenting findings clearly than about deep
technical implementation.

**Typical required skills:** SQL (for pulling and querying data directly),
spreadsheet tools (Excel — pivot tables, formulas), data visualization
tools (Tableau, Power BI), and strong communication skills for writing
reports and presenting to non-technical stakeholders. Basic statistics
(understanding averages, trends, correlation vs. causation) is expected,
but deep machine learning knowledge usually isn't required for this role.

**Typical entry path:** a business, economics, or related degree is
common, though candidates from technical backgrounds also enter this role
when they prefer the business/communication side of data work over deep
technical implementation. Internships in operations, finance, or
analytics roles are a common stepping stone, since the role rewards
business-domain familiarity as much as technical skill.

**What makes someone successful in this role:** the ability to translate
a vague business question ("why did sales drop last quarter?") into a
specific, answerable analysis, and then translate the analysis results
back into a clear recommendation a decision-maker can act on — the
communication loop in both directions matters as much as the analysis
itself.
